When text-back wins

You already answer most calls live; losses concentrate after hours, weekends, and web forms. A text-back catches exactly that overflow — no new phone system, no per-minute billing shock, installed in 7 days in your own Twilio/Sheets.

When it doesn't

You need full call handling, complex dispatch, or legal-intake interrogation — buy a receptionist (human or AI). We'll tell you so on the fit call.
